MSFS 2024 1.8.16.0, Synaptic A220 in the cockpit, one 5120x1440 monitor
Sim window client 2401x1360; the point used is client (720, 1080), on the synoptic display

Which input sequence makes the simulator pop a panel out, sent with SendInput from another
process. Each run listed the simulator's windows before and after.

1. right-alt down, click, right-alt up, after SetForegroundWindow
   foreground before: (another app)
   foreground after raise: Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024
   -> no new window

2. the same, with a plain left click first
   foreground after a plain click: Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024
   -> new window: 'DISPLAYUNITS' 7394x1071

3. the same as 1, with two bare move events instead of the plain click
   (SetCursorPos +1px, MOUSEEVENTF_MOVE, back, MOUSEEVENTF_MOVE)
   -> new window: 'DISPLAYUNITS' 7394x1071

4. as 3, but with right-alt already held while priming
   -> no new window (the point was covered by the existing pop-out; inconclusive)

Reading: the window must be fronted, and the first input after that is swallowed, so
something has to precede the click. A plain click does the job and presses whatever is
under the cursor, which on a display is a button. A bare move event does the job and
presses nothing, so that is what the exerciser sends.

Also observed: a click sent at a point covered by another window goes to that window, so
"is the cursor over the simulator" has to be judged by the simulator window's bounds, not by
what is on top at that point - the exerciser's own window is usually over the spot.
