--- why this matters for the existing scheme ---
  FS Copilot's replay builds `new MouseEvent(type, {bubbles, cancelable})`
  with no clientX/clientY, so both default to 0. Those events DO reach
  WasmSimCanvas and DO become WASM_MOUSE_DOWN — at (0, 0), the corner of
  the screen. The A350 does not ignore FS Copilot's clicks. It receives
  every one of them at the wrong place.

--- inject at (823, 514)   dry run — suppressed
  elementFromPoint  img  (the live-view img — events bubble to the canvas)

  Coherent.call intercepted:
      WASM_MOUSE_DOWN(94, 823, 514, 0)
      WASM_MOUSE_UP(94, 823, 514, 0)
      WASM_CLICK(94, 823, 514, 0)

      Coordinates arrived intact. This is the whole mechanism working:
      a synthetic MouseEvent at (x, y) becomes WASM_MOUSE_DOWN(guid, x, y).
